## KEY RISK SCENARIOS

**1. Dovish Warsh — "One-and-Done" (15-20%)**
If Warsh signals the hike is an insurance move and the Fed will pause to assess, expect a violent reversal: US30 +2-3%, NAS100 +3-5%, gold +3-5%, USD -1-2%. All short conviction trades would be invalidated. This is the single most important binary for the entire dashboard. Probability is low given CPI 3.4% and oil > $100, but not zero — Warsh may prioritise financial stability given 5% yields and UK gilt stress.

**2. Hawkish Warsh — "Start of a Campaign" (60-65%)**
The base case. Warsh signals that one hike is not enough, opens the door to October/December, and emphasises the inflation fight. Equities -2-4%, gold -2-3%, USD +1-2%, 10Y toward 5.25%. All three conviction trades benefit. The dot plot will be scrutinised for the terminal rate projection — anything above 4.25% is hawkish.

**3. Middle East Further Escalation (20-25%)**
Attacks on Saudi infrastructure expand to Strait of Hormuz disruption. Oil +10-15%, equities -3-5%, gold bid re-emerges (first time in weeks). This would complicate the Fed narrative — supply-shock inflation forces hawkishness even as growth fears rise. A true stagflation scenario.

**4. UK Gilt Crisis Accelerates (15-20%)**
30Y gilt yields push above 6.5% ahead of Thursday's BoE. GBP crosses in turmoil. BoE forced into an emergency response — either emergency hike (GBP-positive) or QE resumption (GBP-catastrophe). GBPCAD could swing 200-300 pips either direction.

**5. Warsh Holds Rates Unchanged (5%)**
The 5.5% tail risk. If the Fed holds despite 94.5% market pricing, the credibility damage would be severe. Equities might initially rally (relief) then sell off (confidence loss). Gold could spike on the signal that the Fed is not serious about inflation. Probability is extremely low but the market impact would be extreme.
